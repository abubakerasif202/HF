## Already implemented

- Six independently indexable service pages already have service-specific descriptions, H1s, introductions, sections, FAQs and contextual links. Source: lib/site-data.ts services and app/components/Site.tsx DetailPage. Homepage visibility concentration cannot be explained as missing service pages.
- Self-canonical metadata and social metadata, server-rendered content, Service/FAQ/Breadcrumb schema, CollectionPage/ItemList hubs, unique canonical sitemap and crawlable booking noindex are present.
- Explicit legacy about/contact/blog/interstate redirects, alternate-host canonical redirects and trailing-slash normalization are configured in next.config.ts.
- Homepage image has responsive picture sources and high fetch priority; below-fold photos and map iframe are lazy loaded. Do not assert poor Core Web Vitals without measured evidence.
- Third-party Google ratings are deliberately excluded from MovingCompany aggregateRating markup.

## Remaining opportunities and root causes

1. **Area content differentiation:** lib/hf-service-areas.ts:714–756 uses regional fallback access, introduction, route and largely shared FAQs for areas without localAreaProfiles. Named editorial profiles, overrides and locality references already improve selected areas. Prioritize the remaining pages by actual impressions and intent; verify property/access facts before adding specific copy. Repeating suburb names is not independent evidence of local experience.
2. **Guide depth and attribution:** lib/site-data.ts guideBodies and guides generator provide four short planning sections. This pass replaced the seven guides' shared general-moving FAQs with questions relevant to each checklist; the pricing guide retains its calculation FAQs. Further depth should follow GSC questions and owner-reviewed examples rather than padding pages. Existing pages should receive improvements before new pages are considered.
3. **Business entity completeness:** app/page.tsx defines MovingCompany and WebSite; inner-route schemas refer to those IDs. A shared entity graph can make each page self-contained, although an external ID reference is valid and this alone is not evidence of an indexing or ranking failure. Avoid duplicate entities.
4. **Sitemap update signals:** app/sitemap.ts emits URLs without lastModified. This is valid. Add lastModified only when reliable per-page content dates exist; do not publish fabricated freshness dates.
5. **Evidence gaps:** repository checks cannot determine Google-selected canonicals, indexed status, referring-domain quality, competitors, GBP completeness, page-specific Core Web Vitals or conversion quality. Obtain those reports before diagnosing a visibility cause.
